NABA Black Monitoring Categories

NABA asks all public and third-sector organizations across Scotland to use the following Black ethnicity categories in their monitoring forms, strictly aligned with official Scottish Census standards:

Black Black Scottish Black British African African Scottish African British Caribbean Caribbean Scottish Caribbean British Other Caribbean Background Other African Background

✦ For context, this data ensures proper representation, accurate resource allocation, and civic transparency for African and Black communities across Scottish local authorities and public bodies.

What To Do When Calling 999 or 112

  • Speak clearly and remain calm: The operator will immediately ask which emergency service you require (Police, Ambulance, Fire, or Coastguard).
  • State your exact location: Give your city, street name, building number, or notable landmark (or use the what3words app).
  • Describe the emergency: Clarify if anyone is injured, if an offence is actively taking place, or if anyone is in imminent danger.
  • Stay on the line: Do not hang up until instructed by the operator. Follow all first aid or safety instructions given.
