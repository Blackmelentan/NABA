Education & Civil Rights

Anti-Racism Education with Glasgow City Council

Working hand-in-hand with Glasgow City Council Education Services, NABA has delivered landmark anti-racism and inclusion sessions to over 600 students. In partnership and collaboration with Dr Stuart Lawrence (brother of the late Stephen Lawrence) and Mr Adeyemi Johnson (brother-in-law of the late Sheku Bayoh), we bring lived experience and transformative education into Scottish classrooms.

Represented by Director Pa Lamin Komma and Executive Ambassadors at the Glasgow City Chambers Education Committee, alongside Moving Forward Movement CEO Peter Igbinsa, NABA ensures structural change accompanies classroom dialogue.

Civic Representation

Community Town Hall Meetings

NABA's ongoing Town Hall programme across Glasgow, Edinburgh, and Dundee provides residents with a direct, accountable line to public authorities, local councils, and police divisions.

Following tragic incidents such as the untimely passing of Dr. Fortune Gomo in Dundee, NABA actively facilitated high-level meetings with Police Scotland leadership to ensure community protection, transparent communication, and compassionate support for affected families.

Heritage & Culture

Cultural Festivals & Celebrations

"Inclusivity is our strength." NABA proudly marches and co-produces major multicultural celebrations throughout the country. From the Dundee City Council Multi Cultural Festival to the annual Gambian Cultural Weekend in Edinburgh and Sierra Leone Independence Celebrations, our heritage is vibrantly represented in Scotland's cultural calendar.

These festivals unite hundreds of families, showcase traditional cuisine, authentic attire, musical performances, and create generational bonds for young Scottish Africans.
