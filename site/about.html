About Us

We are an engaged community

The National African & Black Association (NABA) is a UK non-profit dedicated to the legacy of Sheku Bayoh.

What Is NABA?

One community, one strength

NABA is a Scottish-based, not-for-profit organisation dedicated to promoting equity, inclusion, and improved access to public services for African and Black individuals across Scotland.

We work in partnership with institutions to assess the impact of policies and practices, deliver cultural awareness and anti-racism training, and foster meaningful engagement between public services and the communities they serve.

We are led by the communities we serve, working city by city Glasgow, Edinburgh, Dundee, Aberdeen, Falkirk and beyond so that support reflects the place and the people asking for it.

Listed as a member organisation of the Scottish Council for Voluntary Organisations (SCVO).

Recent News

Working with Glasgow City Council

Education Committee

Anti-racism in Education, with Dr Stuart Lawrence

NABA, in partnership and collaboration with Dr Stuart Lawrence (brother of the late Stephen Lawrence) and Mr Adeyemi Johnson (brother-in-law of the late Sheku Bayoh), delivered a session to over 600 students about inclusion and anti-racism. NABA was represented by Director Pa Lamin Komma and Executive Ambassadors at the Glasgow City Chambers Education committee meeting, alongside Moving Forward Movement CEO Peter Igbinsa.
