New Initiative Launching 2026

A hub built for the next generation

Mentorship, a curated resource library, leadership development, and real funding routes — brought together in one place for young people across the diaspora, and the mentors and partners who want to back them.

Find a mentor → Browse resources

What The Hub Offers

Four pillars

1:1 Mentorship

Matched with a volunteer mentor from the community for guidance on education, career, or simply finding your feet.

Digital Skills & Resources

A curated library of real, vetted organisations covering education, careers, wellbeing, rights, and housing.

Leadership Programme

Structured pathways for young people to lead their own community projects, with NABA backing and visibility.

Global Exchange

Ties to the Edinburgh–Lagos road trip and diaspora partners — connecting Scottish-based youth to peers across West Africa.

Funding & Partners

Help us fund it

The Youth Hub is a new NABA initiative and is actively seeking funding partners. These are real, active Scottish funding routes we're exploring — if your organisation funds youth work, we'd love to talk.

National

National Lottery Community Fund

The UK's largest community funder, including programmes for community leadership and youth-led change.

 Scotland

The Robertson Trust — Youth Fund

Core funding up to £50,000/year for organisations working with young people aged 14–25.

 Scotland

Corra Foundation

Manages multiple Scottish Government and independent funds, including the Boost Fund for grassroots groups.

 Directory

YouthLink Scotland

National funding directory listing hundreds of live youth work grants across Scotland.

 Scotland

Foundation Scotland

Scotland's community foundation, connecting local funds to grassroots organisations.

 Scotland

Youth Scotland

Grassroots youth work funds including Safe Spaces and Rural Action grants.
