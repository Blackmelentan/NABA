Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to making this site usable by everyone.

Last updated: 2026Standard: WCAG 2.1 AA (target)

Our commitment

NABA exists to make services easier to reach for everyone, and that includes this website. We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines across every page.

Built-in accessibility features

  • A high-contrast mode and adjustable text size, available from the toolbar on the right of every page.
  • Full keyboard navigation, with a visible focus outline on every interactive element.
  • Descriptive alt text on images and clear, consistent heading structure.
  • A "Get Help Now" quick-contact button on every page for anyone who needs direct support.
  • Support for browser and OS-level reduced-motion settings.

Known limitations

We're a small team improving this site in stages. If you hit a page, form, or document that isn't accessible to you, please tell us — we treat every report seriously and will fix it or provide the information another way.

Get in touch

Email admin@nabaorg.uk or call 07990 753498 to report an accessibility issue or request information in an alternative format.
