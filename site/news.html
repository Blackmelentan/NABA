Education Committee

Working in Partnership and Collaboration with Glasgow City Council Education

Working with Glasgow City Council promoting anti-racism in Education. NABA, in partnership and collaboration with Dr Stuart Lawrence (brother of the late Stephen Lawrence) and Mr Adeyemi Johnson (brother-in-law of the late Sheku Bayoh), delivered a remarkable session to over 600 students about inclusion and anti-racism. NABA was also represented by Director Pa Lamin Komma and Executive Ambassadors at the Glasgow City Chambers Education committee meeting, alongside Moving Forward Movement CEO Peter Igbinsa.
