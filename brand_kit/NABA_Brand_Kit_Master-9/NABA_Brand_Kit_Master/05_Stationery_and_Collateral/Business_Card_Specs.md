# NABA Business Card Specifications

**Size:** 85 × 55 mm (standard UK/EU business card)  
**Bleed:** 3 mm all sides recommended for print  
**Safe area:** 5 mm from edges

## Front
- Background: Solid Deep Onyx `#111111`
- Mark: Exact original lion head + amber-gold triangle, centered
- Optional: Subtle metallic gold foil effect on triangle edges for premium print runs
- No text on front (pure mark impact)

## Back
- Background: Pure White or Warm Off-White `#FAF8F5`
- Top: Exact original double-line “NABA” wordmark (or full lockup scaled)
- Contact block (centered or left-aligned):
  - admin@nabaorg.uk
  - +44 7990 753 498
  - www.nabaorg.uk
- QR code (bottom right or centered) linking to nabaorg.uk or membership page
- Thin amber `#F5A623` accent line or small triangle motif
- Footer: “National African & Black Association • Scotland & UK”

## Print Notes
- Use the locked original logo files only
- CMYK conversion of `#F5A623` ≈ C0 M40 Y90 K0 (test on press)
- Black should be rich black for solid areas: C40 M40 Y40 K100 or pure K
