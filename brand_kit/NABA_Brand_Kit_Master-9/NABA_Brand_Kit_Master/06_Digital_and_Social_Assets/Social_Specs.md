# NABA Social Media Asset Specs

## Profile Avatars
- Instagram / Facebook / LinkedIn / X (Twitter): Square 400×400 px minimum (upload higher)
- Preferred: Exact original lion + triangle mark centered on `#111111` or pure white
- Avoid full lockup with long tagline at small avatar sizes

## Header / Cover Banners
- X (Twitter): 1500 × 500 px
- LinkedIn: 1584 × 396 px
- Facebook: 820 × 312 px (or 1200 × 630 for posts)
- Recommended style: Deep Onyx `#111111` base, large centered or left-aligned original mark, supporting text “National African & Black Association • Scotland & UK”, subtle geometric gold accents or soft light rays

## Post Templates
- Use brand colours only
- Always include clear space around the locked logo
- Gold accent bars or triangle motifs for visual rhythm
